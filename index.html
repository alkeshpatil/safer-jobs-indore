Rules: CCPA and the consumer court route
  • CCPA coaching guidelines. "Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisement in Coaching Sector, 2024", issued around 13 November 2024 (PIB release PRID=2073013 via search result; Medianama article dated 14 Nov 2024 fetched). Per Medianama: coaching centre = institution providing coaching to more than 50 students; bans unverified success-rate claims, assured job placements, unverified high-score claims, exaggerated course/faculty claims and false urgency ("limited seats"); disclaimers must be clear in font size and position; written consent before using a student's name, photo or testimonial (and only after the student's success); every centre must be linked with the National Consumer Helpline; endorsers are liable. Violations are a contravention of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. medianama.com/2024/11/223-ccpa-new-guidel… (secondary source). PIB itself returned HTTP 403 on fetch.
  • Earlier advisory. Search result says CCPA told coaching institutes in January 2024 not to claim "100% selection" or "100% job guarantee". news.careers360.com/coaching-institutes-s… (secondary source)
  • Enforcement numbers (conflicting). One report: 45 notices and Rs 61.6 lakh penalty; another: Rs 54.6 lakh on 18 institutes; April 2025 report: 49 notices and Rs 77.60 lakh on 24 coaching centres. Cases named: Career Line Coaching, Sikar (Rs 5 lakh) and Sriram's IAS (concealing which course successful candidates took). These are UPSC-type exam coaching, not job-skills institutes. Sources: bestmediainfo.com/mediainfo/mediainfo-mar… , etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/ccpa-asks-coachi… .
  • 2026 CCPA edtech action. March 2026: Raising Superstars Enterprises fined Rs 8 lakh over child-development claims; CCPA said after-the-fact disclaimers do not cure precise, prominent outcome claims. Not about jobs, but the principle applies to small-print on guarantees. courtkutchehry.com/blog/ccpa-penalty-rais… (secondary source)
  • No CCPA order on job-placement guarantees found in 2025-26 in this session's searches. Whether the 2024 coaching guidelines cover IT/skills training institutes (vs exam coaching) is not verified.
  • Consumer commission route works. Gurdaspur District Consumer Commission, Punjab, ruling dated 16 September 2026 (Law Trend, 23 Sep 2026): a 6-month online AI-in-product-management course, "100% placement guarantee", fee Rs 84,960; student certified 24 Nov 2025 and not placed. Commission held advertising a guarantee and delivering only interview facilitation is an unfair trade practice. Refund Rs 84,960 + Rs 10,000 harassment + Rs 5,000 costs. The firm must show clearly disclosed limiting terms before enrolment or proof it met its obligation (job offer letters, interview records); it had none. Firm not named by Law Trend (one other outlet's headline names Eduinx; unverified). lawtrend.in/consumer-panel-orders-educati… (secondary source). Online course, Punjab: it is a precedent for how a commission reasons, not an Indore ruling.

Fine-print patterns to watch for
  • Refund is partial more often than full: WsCube 50%, AnalytixLabs 50% (search result only), Kraftshala 60%; full refund only at Internshala and Jobaaj.
  • "Placed" can mean an internship (Internshala ≥ ₹10,000/month stipend; WsCube includes paid internships).
  • Window start differs: Internshala 6 months after graduating the course; Jobaaj 3 months after placement assistance starts; WsCube 12 months from completion; AnalytixLabs 6 months from placement window.
  • Typical void clauses: attendance (WsCube 90%), applications per week (Internshala 10), refusing offers over location or salary (WsCube), missed fees or EMI (Internshala, WsCube), finding a job on your own (WsCube).
  • A third-party review blog claims fewer than 5% of eligible students claimed refunds across three courses it investigated; small sample, source quality unclear (search result only).
  • Salary floors are rare: Kraftshala ₹4.5 LPA, Masai ₹5 LPA threshold (2021). Internshala and WsCube promise none.
  • Clauses to get in writing before paying: the exact refund percentage and deadline, the definition of "placed", the minimum pay, whether internships count, what counts as refusing an offer, and the start date of the placement window.
